text: |-
  In “Intelligence on Demand” we investigate the integration of Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI tools in modern design processes, uncovering the potential and challenges these technologies pose in shaping the future of communication design. This thesis serves as an exploration where AI is not merely a tool but a transformative agent, reshaping our creative processes.
  Addressing critical issues like data privacy, copyright, and ethical dilemmas, it emphasizes the necessity for open-source ideologies and collaborative development.

  The creation of diagrams, illustrations and original artifacts in this thesis serves as a platform for dialogue and reflection. These visual elements, coupled with a thorough investigation enriched with historical images from design publications, magazines and websites, aim to engage both designers and the broader audience, inviting them to explore the implications of AI in design and to consider their role in its future.

  Relatore: Salvatore Zingale
