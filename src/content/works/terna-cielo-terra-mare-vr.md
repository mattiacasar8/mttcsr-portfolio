  Terna “Cielo Terra Mare VR” is an immersive and multisensory platform designed to communicate the initiatives and projects of Terna, Europe’s largest independent electricity transmission operator. Divided into three main environments — Sky, Earth, Sea — this digital experience takes the visitor on a guided journey through Terna’s commitment to sustainability.

  Starting from an already innovative web experience, the challenge was to transition it into virtual reality. In a project marked by strong experimentation, we pushed the technical limits of the Meta Quest to design a space that allows users to engage with Terna’s sustainability projects in a meticulously crafted scenic space. With precise attention to color, music, sounds and shapes, we achieved a high level of immersion using three.js, WebXR and environments designed in Blender.

  With a robust storytelling element, 3D animations and environmental sounds, visitors are led through data and information, highlighting Terna’s strategic role in building a more sustainable future.

  In collaboration with: The Visual Agency, E. Monfilis, S. Piccolomini.
