text: |-
  For the Eni Agri Hub project, we tackled the challenge of demystifying a complex subject matter for a wider audience. Our vision was to transcend the typical jargon-heavy approach and instead invite the audience into a story — one that unfolds one discovery at a time. We crafted a content architecture that favored narrative clarity over encyclopedic information, presented in four self-contained yet interconnected sections.

  This thematic division was key to simplifying complex concepts while delving deep into specific topics, making them transparent and captivating for the visitor. To enhance the user experience, we integrated interactive 3D totems as thematic pillars. These models not only provided context but also delivered that “wow” effect, fostering an immersive and memorable journey through the narrative of innovation.

  With rich use of photos, maps, infographics and 3D models, the project is a testament to the power of clear structure and creative visual storytelling in making intricate topics accessible and engaging.

  In collaboration with: The Visual Agency, J. Moreschi, E. Monfilis, S. Piccolomini.
