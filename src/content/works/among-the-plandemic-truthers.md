text: |-
  Starting in 2020, to take action against false or possibly harmful medical misinformation, the main social media websites started to take down misleading content or ban the authors of these posts. As a result, many users decided to flock to alternative websites that promised them to be censor-free and real hubs of free speech.

  “Among the Plandemic Truthers” is a data report that analyses how misinformation and disinformation spread out in these dark platforms. After gathering data from various dark platforms we used different tools to clean, sort and make sense of the large quantity of information we found. With applications like Gephi we were able to map different kinds of networks and display our findings in this report.

  In collaboration with: D. Chiappini, A. De Simone, E. Ghebaur, F. Gheli, H. Ma, R. Riccardelli.
