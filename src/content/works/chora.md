text: >-
  Collaborating with The Visual Agency and teammates, we embarked on a mission
  with Chora Media to carve out their audience away from the mainstream podcast
  platforms. Their aim was to create an exclusive space for their high-quality
  podcast library, leading to the birth of the Chora app, an innovative portal
  for high-quality podcast listening experiences.


  The app reflects a journalistic approach, blending elements of online
  publishing. We integrated custom graphics for timely journalistic events and
  designed a user-friendly system to bookmark favorite content. It prioritizes
  the audio experience with engaging subtitles and social sharing features that
  let users post standout quotes with a tap.


  Ambitious in scope, the app introduces visual indicators for tracking listened
  content, alongside icons and animations. The project is a showcase of design
  that makes high-quality audio not just available but compelling, and a
  pleasure to engage with in a personal space crafted for the community.


  In collaboration with: The Visual Agency, S. Piccolomini, F. Papini, M.
  Bonera.
