text: |-
  Starting from a brief given to us by UNESCO and “decenniodelmare”, we designed an interactive and iterative event aimed at high school students to talk about the future of our oceans. But how can we talk about climate and future avoiding the feeling of “eco-anxiety”, and instead spark interest and curiosity in the young generation’s minds?

  The main focus of the project was the design of an interactive canvas that the students could interact with during a series of talks. This shared space, visible on LED walls between talks, was developed to give the audience a space to express their feelings in a familiar, fun and also innovative way, like using emotes during a live Twitch stream. The feedback gives the presenter and organizers an immediate sense of how the crowd is reacting to different topics, to comment on it together with the students.

  Once we developed this ever-changing canvas, we used various combinations of its elements to create a communication campaign for the selected cities and online.

  This project was done during the “Design degli Eventi” course of Politecnico di Milano with prof. Luca Fois.
  In collaboration with: A. De Simone, L. Urietti, V. Zaccaria.
