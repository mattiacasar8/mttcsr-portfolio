text: |-
  We designed and prototyped the only service for smart-workers that allows the growth of micro communities, thanks to partnerships with local and private businesses.

  Can we combine the flexibility of working remotely with the quality of a shared workspace to bring together talented people and useful services? With Splace we tried to answer this question, with an app that would allow us to do exactly that. We took inspiration from several successful apps to integrate the discovery of new places, the creation of a professional profile, and services such as lunches or activities to be carried out during and after the working day.

  In addition to those looking for spaces to work, we also thought about the partners who make their premises available, mapping their needs to propose solutions in their interface. Reviews, menus, user and activity management are some of the details we focused on.

  In collaboration with: V. Andrini, A. D’Alessandro, D. Chiappini, F. Gheli, R. Galli, R. Riccardelli.
