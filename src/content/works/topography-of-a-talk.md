  At the beginning of 2025, The Visual Agency launched the Digital Talks, a series of conversations with designers and industry professionals. Classic situation: great speakers and no budget. To reward them, we turned each talk into a gift through what we do best, data visualization: a generative, data-driven poster for every speaker.

  What was said becomes a landscape. A custom text analysis tool built on Claude processes the talk transcription, strips filler words and maps meaningful terms into a network of nodes. Word frequency defines the height of each mountain, conceptual proximity forms mountain ranges, while isolated ideas stand as single peaks.

  My part was the procedural system behind it. I connected the text analysis scripts directly to Blender, so each network is turned into terrain without manual modelling: posters can be generated in quick succession, almost in real time, and a new talk becomes a new landscape as soon as its transcription is ready.

  How it landed becomes colour. At the end of each talk the audience answers two quick questions about immediate impact and future intention. The dominant answers generate one of 16 colour gradients, designed for accessibility and contrast, that wash over the 3D geometry. The structure represents what was said, the colour how it was perceived.

  The project won Gold at the Indigo Awards 2026 and was exhibited at Digital Design Days in Milan.

  In collaboration with: The Visual Agency, F. Gheli, B. Bazzan.
